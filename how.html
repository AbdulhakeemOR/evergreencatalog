ground rules.

Go ahead

Adapt a kit for your own project, change the wording and visuals, and share your finished work with your audience.

Please don't

Resell or redistribute the original, unmodified kit as a template, or claim someone else's work as your own.

Always check the actual license that comes with a real template before using it.

A few common questions

Good to know.

Are these real, ready-to-download kits?＋

No. This catalog is a sample project. The kits demonstrate how a template library could be organized, and the “Get this kit” button shows a demo message.

Can I use these kits for client work?＋

The kits shown here are sample content, not downloadable assets. For a real kit, check its specific license before using it for personal or client work.

What tools do I need to customize a kit?＋

Each kit lists its formats, such as Docs or Canva. You would need access to the matching tool to edit a real template.

Why are some kits available in more than one language?＋

Some content works best when it can meet people in their language. Language availability is listed on every kit card and detail page.
